Why come

  • The real climbs: Koppenberg, Paterberg and Oude Kwaremont, the same hills as the Ronde, without the peloton on your wheel.
  • A legendary stop at café 't Juiste Verzet, on the slope of the Oude Kwaremont.
  • Watch the Ronde the next day with a connoisseur's eye, and tired legs.
  • A friendly group: no bib, no timing, we regroup at the top of every climb and nobody is left behind.

What to bring

  • Road bike in perfect condition (a service is advised)
  • Wide tyres (28 mm or more) to soak up the cobbles
  • Helmet (compulsory)
  • 2 inner tubes, tyre levers, pump or CO₂
  • Windproof jacket and clothes for Flemish weather
  • Your own food (bars, gels, 2 bottles)
  • Bank card or cash for coffee and fries
  • Charged phone with the Strava route downloaded

Getting there and back

  • Meeting point: Marcq-en-Barœul (59700). The exact spot is sent to registered riders on WhatsApp.
  • Public transport: Ilévia network (ilevia.fr). From Lille-Flandres or Lille-Europe station, ride to the start.
  • By car: no dedicated car park announced, so arrive early and carpool if you can.
  • The ride ends where it starts, in Marcq-en-Barœul. No return shuttle is announced.

Safety

  • Helmet compulsory, bike serviced.
  • Follow the traffic rules in France and Belgium.
  • This is not a race: no timing, we regroup before each climb.
  • Emergencies: call 112 (valid in France and Belgium).
  • Friendly ride: everyone rides at their own responsibility and checks their personal insurance.

FAQ

Is it paid?

No, it is free. Just bring money for your coffee, fries and abbey beer.

What level do I need?

You should be able to ride 100 km comfortably and be ready to do 141 km with 1,374 m of climbing and steep cobbles. The pace is friendly and the group regroups before each climb.

Can I come on an e-bike or gravel bike?

A road bike with wide tyres is recommended. For other bikes, tell the organiser when you register.

What if it rains?

Wet cobbles make the climbs slippery, that is the charm of the Ronde. Bring a windproof jacket and spare clothes. The decision to go ahead or not is announced the day before.

Is there a support car or mechanic?

To be confirmed. Bring inner tubes, a pump and tools to be self-sufficient.

How do I get back to Lille?

The ride finishes where it starts, in Marcq-en-Barœul.
